Good type is *rarely noticed*, but a **missing glyph** is. Names such as Zoë, Søren, Łukasz, Dvořák and Núñez need Latin-1 and Latin Extended-A, while “curly quotes”, en dashes (2020–2026) and an ellipsis… live in the core set.

Café menus say *crème brûlée*, **œuvre** and ***déjà vu*** in one breath. Polish: Zażółć gęślą jaźń. Czech: Příliš žluťoučký kůň. Turkish: İstanbul, ığdır, şeker.

## Numbers and code

Prices like €12.50, 3 × 4 = 12 and `inline code` sit next to prose. System fonts draw 日本語 and 한국어 — those stay with the operating system.
